01Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Carrom Studio (the "App", Android package com.kodevengers.carrom), developed and published by Kodevengers ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information when you use the App.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

02Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, and does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. No information about you or your use of the App is sent to us or to anyone else.

03Data stored on your device

To remember your preferences and progress, the App saves a small amount of data locally on your device only:

  • Player name, if you choose to enter one. It is used only to label your side of the board.
  • Game settings, such as sound, volume, haptics, game mode, match length, AI difficulty and visual preferences.
  • Game statistics, such as matches won and lost against the AI, local matches played and practice results.
This data never leaves your device, and we have no access to it.

04Device permissions

The App does not request access to sensitive device features or data, including location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

05Sharing and third parties

Because we do not collect any information, we do not sell, rent, trade or otherwise share personal data with any third party. The App contains no third-party advertising or analytics services.

The App is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information under its own privacy policy when you download or update apps; that processing is outside the App and our control.

06Children's privacy

The App is suitable for players of all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

07Your choices

You are always in control of the data stored by the App. You can change your player name and settings at any time from within the App. To remove all App data, clear the App's storage in your device settings or uninstall the App.

08Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
